Who are we?

The University of Sheffield's Horror Society is a place for freaks, creeps and generally unsettling people to gather and share their love for all things horror!

We offer weekly film nights, spooky/scary events and our annual production of The Rocky Horror Picture Show!

As of this coming year, Horror Society will be celebrating its 10th anniversary!

Meet our committee

What do we do?

Film Nights

Every Friday evening, we meet for our weekly film social! We show a wide variety of horror films, from classics to more niche favourites, and we usually have a theme for each month of films.

Before the film, we meet in the Student Union's Bar One for a drink and some chat, which is a great time to meet your fellow members.

After the film, we usually have a chat about what we watched and then head out to another bar to finish the night.

Be sure to look at our event calendar to see our upcoming films!

Special Events

Throughout the year, we also host a variety of other special events, sometimes alongside other societies. Expect themed film nights, special effects and crafts workshops, a ghost walk and more!

Rocky Horror Production

By far our biggest event of the year is our shadowcast production of The Rocky Horror Picture Show! We have both cast and crew roles available for members to get involved in, or you can just come along and watch. Stay tuned for more details coming soon about our 2027 production.
